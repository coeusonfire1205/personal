25 OCTOBER 2026 · OUR LITTLE BEGINNING

Babyyyy ❤️

You know, if someone had told me a few weeks ago that a simple conversation with a girl on Hinge would turn into something this beautiful, I probably would've laughed it off. But then you happened, and somehow, without me even realising it, you became one of the most beautiful parts of my life.

From calling you Miss Lawyer, to waking up at 7 in the morning just to see you, to our random conversations, your little nakhre, your endless stories and that confidence of yours that I absolutely adore — somewhere between all of this, I started falling for you. ❤️

And you know what I love the most about you? It's how effortlessly you are yourself. That little girl who's so loved and pampered at home, yet carries herself with so much confidence. The old-school soul who loves qawwalis and traditions, but has this beautifully modern way of looking at life. The girl who can talk about absolutely anything and somehow make even the most ordinary conversation feel special.

I don't know exactly when it happened, but I started looking forward to your messages, your calls, your random updates about your day, and even the smallest things you wanted to tell me. And somewhere along the way, your happiness started mattering to me just as much as my own.

I know we're just beginning our story, and there's still so much left to discover about each other. There'll be beautiful days, silly arguments, misunderstandings, and moments when things won't be perfect. But I want you to know one thing — I'm not someone who wants to disappear when things get difficult. I want to stay, understand you, choose you, and figure things out together.

I don't want to promise you a fairytale where everything is perfect. I want something much more real. I want the random drives, the late-night conversations, the stupid jokes, the comfortable silences, the little celebrations, and all those ordinary days that become extraordinary simply because you're there.

And baby, I might not always be the best at expressing everything I feel, but I genuinely want to make you feel as loved, appreciated, and special as you deserve to be.

You came into my life so unexpectedly, yet now the thought of having you by my side feels so beautifully right.

Here's to us, to our little beginning, and to all the memories we haven't made yet. I hope that years from now, we'll look back at our first 7 AM date and smile at how something so simple turned into something so precious.

And if there's one thing I want you to never have to question, it's how much you mean to me.

♡

I LOVE YOU,
BABYYYYY.
